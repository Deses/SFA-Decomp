# DLL 650 / 651 boundary and constant ownership

The eight `dll_28B_*Handler*` functions formerly appended to DLL 650 belong
with DLL 651's state machine. They now precede that unit's existing callbacks,
in retail function order. Both numbered slots and generated source paths are
preserved; no new TU or compiler override is introduced.

## Evidence

DLL 650's last callback ends at EN `802239A4`, where the first 651 substate
handler begins. Only DLL 651 installs these eight functions into its state and
substate tables. Their accesses describe 651's larger state, not the
EarthWalker's `0x660`-byte state.

Retail small data independently separates the units:

- `803E6CD8..803E6CEC` holds the EarthWalker's packed move-blend word and four
  scalar parameters. Only its ten callbacks consume them.
- After natural alignment, `803E6CF0..803E6D20` holds the follower's distance
  thresholds, timer threshold, conversion double, and subsequent literals.
  The handlers and existing 651 callbacks consume this complete pool.

Compiling the corrected units naturally emits these two eight-byte-aligned
pools: 20 and 48 bytes. The old 650 split had claimed the middle 24 bytes of
651's pool while leaving both prefixes unowned. No artificial padding or
extra constant definition is needed. The nine pre-existing 651 callbacks
follow its eight handlers, with its own descriptor retained at the end.

The EarthWalker descriptor retains its already exact earlier position before
the update function's jump tables. Its `.data` extent is 124 bytes; the four
following zeros are linker alignment before the 651 descriptor. Both
descriptors and all neighboring data keep their retail addresses.

## Source reconstruction

Each unit defines its own named constants and reads them through their
addresses, preserving the pool without duplicate literal copies. The former
cross-unit `extern` placeholders are removed. This follows the
[bouncy-crate recovery](sdata2_pool_order.md#bouncy-crate-constant-ownership-2026-09-29).

Moving the handlers exposes their actual compiler context: 651 retains its
existing optimization profile. The velocity-magnitude expression explicitly
rounds both squared components to `f32` before addition, preserving retail's
two multiplies and separate add. Without those casts, its profile contracts
one product into a fused multiply-add. No compiler settings change.

The handlers now use `Dll28BState` from their owning header. Its previously
opaque word at `0xABC` is the random idle timer. The unrelated EarthWalker
header loses the redundant `Dll28BAiState` overlay and the handler declarations.
The retained state size is the allocation-backed `0xAC4`, rather than the old
overlay's unevidenced `0xAC8`; assertions cover the timer and route offsets.

## Verification

All five versions preserve all 27 exact functions and all data in both units:
650 has ten functions and 144 data bytes; 651 has seventeen functions and
168 data bytes. `ninja all_source` and the native strict retail checksum pass.
The 44-byte autogenerated pool gap disappears, leaving 268 unscored data bytes
per version. The generated-path audit confirms that slots 650 and 651 retain
their existing folders and filenames.
Regional projection reproduces both complete split blocks in all four
secondary versions. The retail operand audit confirms the twelve named
constants and literals in these pools, their bytes, and fifteen paired
r2-relative references per secondary version.
